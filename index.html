About Me

Hello! My name is Hongyi Su. I am a PhD candidate at the Autorité de sûreté nucléaire et de radioprotection (ASNR) in Fontenay-aux-Roses, France, within the Bureau d’évaluation des risques sismiques pour la sûreté des installations (BERSSIN) , in collaboration with the ISTerre laboratory at Université Grenoble Alpes.

My research focuses on understanding earthquake source processes and improving the prediction and simulation of strong ground motion. My current doctoral research investigates near-fault ground motion, site effects, and wave propagation at the regional scale for a shallow earthquake in southeastern France. I am particularly interested in combining observational data, physics-based numerical simulations, and statistical methods to better understand earthquake ground motion.

My broader research interests include earthquake source mechanics, dynamic rupture modelling, computational seismology, high-performance computing, seismic data analysis, and physics-based ground motion modelling.

Research Projects

Near-Fault Ground Motion and Site Effects

My current PhD research focuses on understanding and predicting near-fault ground motion for shallow earthquakes in southeastern France. The project investigates the effects of earthquake source processes, local site conditions, and regional wave propagation on observed ground motion.

Near-Fault Ground Motion Site Effects Wave Propagation Ground Motion Prediction Bayesian Methods

2019 Le Teil Earthquake — Ground Motion Reconstruction

I am developing a Bayesian framework for reconstructing near-fault ground motion from in-situ observations, with a focus on the 2019 Le Teil earthquake in southeastern France. The work combines observational constraints with statistical and physical modelling approaches to improve our understanding of near-source ground motion.

Le Teil Earthquake Bayesian Framework Seismic Observations Ground Motion Reconstruction

Dynamic Rupture Modelling of the 2017 Botswana Earthquake

During my Master's research, I investigated the 2017 Mw 6.5 Botswana intraplate earthquake using physics-based dynamic rupture simulations with the SeisSol software. The project explored earthquake source processes and their influence on simulated ground motion.

Dynamic Rupture SeisSol Physics-Based Simulation Intraplate Earthquake Computational Seismology

Seismic Investigation of the Mount Meager Geothermal System

As a PURE undergraduate researcher at the University of Calgary, I participated in a seismic investigation of the Mount Meager geothermal system in British Columbia, Canada. The project involved seismic data analysis and structure imaging to investigate the subsurface characteristics of the geothermal area.

Seismic Data Analysis Geothermal Exploration Structure Imaging Mount Meager

PSD Analysis for Seismic Data

My undergraduate thesis investigated Power Spectral Density (PSD) characteristics of seismic data from the Mount Meager area. The project focused on analysing seismic signals and extracting information relevant to subsurface and seismic noise characteristics.

PSD Analysis Seismic Data Signal Processing
